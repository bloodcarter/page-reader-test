Payroll that runs itself

Acme Payroll pays teams in 40 countries from one dashboard. Each pay run calculates gross pay, deductions and local taxes, then files the returns that each country requires. Finance teams approve a run once, and Acme moves the money on the scheduled date.

Setting up takes about a week. You connect your accounting system, invite employees to enter their bank and tax details, and choose a pay schedule for each country. Acme checks every record before the first run and flags anything a local tax office would reject.

Pricing

Plans start at 12 dollars per employee per month for domestic payroll. International payroll costs 39 dollars per employee per month and includes filings, year-end forms and a named specialist for each country.

Security

Acme holds a SOC 2 Type II report, encrypts data at rest and in transit, and keeps payroll records in the region where each employee works. Customers can request the report under a mutual NDA.
